# LexChain Product Context

## Register

Professional legal document platform.

## Target users

Lawyers and clients who manage, sign, verify, and retrieve legal and notarial documents.

## Product purpose

Help users manage document workflows and identify the right legal record with clear, trustworthy information.

## Brand personality

Professional, clear, dependable, and calm.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered or marketing-style layouts.
- Opaque search results that show technical IDs without useful record details.

## Strategic design principles

- Keep document identity and the next action easy to recognize.
- Make exact register lookup clear and require every part of the reference.
- Keep controls and validation accessible by keyboard and screen reader.
- Preserve backend ownership of document access and authorization.
